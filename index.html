// About Me

Hello, I'm PixelArt

Sometimes I look back and smile at how far I've come. I started photography from scratch — no fancy gear, no studio, just pure passion and curiosity. I remember the early days of experimenting, making mistakes, learning little by little, and falling in love with the process of capturing moments.

Step by step, through dedication and consistency, I've grown from those humble beginnings into a professional photographer. And I'm still growing — pushing myself to create even more stunning, creative, and timeless images for every client who trusts me.
